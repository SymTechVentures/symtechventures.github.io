Five divisions

Five frontiers

Five divisions, one SYMMETRIA-based mission.

SymTech Ventures is organised around five proposed research divisions: SymCore Energy, SymCraft Nano, SymCura Health, SymSpace Dynamics and SymLogic Compute. Each division represents a different pathway for developing future prototype systems that fully utilise the SYMMETRIA Unified Field Framework concept.
