SYMMETRIA

A proposed unified-field framework exploring symmetry, nilpotent algebra, layered fields, computation and long-term scientific possibility.

Framework

What it is

SYMMETRIA is not presented as a completed or validated theory. It is a structured research direction: a way of organising mathematical ideas, physical questions, computational models and long-term technology speculation around the central theme of symmetry.

The aim is to move from broad ideas toward clearer definitions, internal consistency tests, computational modelling and external critique.
