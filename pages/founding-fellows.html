Founding Fellows

SymTech Ventures may eventually seek five founding leads, one for each division, to help shape the project with discipline, credibility and long-term vision.

Not ordinary job vacancies

This is an early-stage concept, not a funded company with salaried roles. The Founding Fellows idea is about identifying thoughtful people who might help guide the direction of the project as it matures.

The ideal people would be technically curious, scientifically cautious and comfortable working with ambitious ideas without overstating them.

Energy Lead

Energy systems, infrastructure and safety thinking.

Nano Lead

Materials, fabrication and semiconductor awareness.

Health Lead

Biomedical caution, ethics and clinical realism.

Space Lead

Aerospace systems, simulation and long-horizon planning.

Compute Lead

AI, simulation, software and modelling infrastructure.
