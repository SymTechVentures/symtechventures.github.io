SYMMETRIA Discussion Series

A fifteen-paper exploratory discussion series introducing the SYMMETRIA framework, its conceptual foundations, its architecture, its research methodology, and its long-term scientific vision.

Why this series exists

The SYMMETRIA Discussion Series introduces the motivation, language, architecture and scientific philosophy behind the framework. Each paper builds upon the previous one, gradually developing the concepts, assumptions and research questions that underpin SYMMETRIA.

The aim is not to present SYMMETRIA as an experimentally validated theory, but to clarify what the framework is attempting to explore, why it matters, and how its ideas may be investigated through structured theory-building, mathematical modelling, computational simulation and critical review.
